2. APP PURPOSE, TARGET AUDIENCE & VALUE:
• Purpose: PTE Ninja is a mobile exam preparation and AI diagnostic practice application for the Pearson Test of English Academic (PTE Academic).
• Target Audience: International students, professionals, and visa applicants preparing for English proficiency certification.
• Problem Solved & Value Provided: Traditional PTE coaching is expensive and provides slow feedback. PTE Ninja provides learners with 20+ exam task types across Speaking, Writing, Reading, and Listening, delivering instant diagnostic feedback on pronunciation, oral fluency, grammar, vocabulary, and content accuracy on the 10–90 PTE scale, plus personalized study plans and mock tests.

3. INSTRUCTIONS FOR ACCESSING MAIN FEATURES & LOGIN CREDENTIALS:
• Option A (Instant Guest - No Login Required): Tap "Continue as Guest" / "Instant Guest" on the welcome screen for immediate access to the dashboard and practice question banks.
• Option B (Sign in with Apple / Email): Tap "Sign in with Apple" or sign up / sign in with any email and password.
• How to Test Speaking / AI Scoring: From the Dashboard, select "Speaking" -> "Read Aloud" or "Repeat Sentence" -> Tap the Microphone button (grant mic permission) -> Speak the prompt -> Tap Stop/Submit to view the instant AI Scorecard.
• How to Test VIP Subscriptions: Tap "Upgrade" or "VIP" in the top bar or Profile tab to open the VIP paywall and trigger native Apple StoreKit 2 auto-renewable subscriptions or Offer Code redemption.
• How to Test Account Deletion: Open the "Profile" tab -> Tap the red Trash Can icon (next to the Log Out button) at the top-right of the user profile card -> Confirm deletion.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES, TOOLS & PLATFORMS USED:
• Authentication & User Identity: Firebase Authentication (supporting Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, Email/Password, and Anonymous Guest Auth).
• Database & Storage: PostgreSQL (Neon Serverless Postgres) for storing question banks, user practice history, study schedules, and vocabulary progress.
• AI & Speech Evaluation Services: Google Gemini API / Google Cloud Vertex AI (accessed securely via our HTTPS backend server) to analyze learner speech audio and written essays for pronunciation, fluency, and grammar scoring.
• Payment Processing (iOS): Native Apple StoreKit 2 (In-App Purchases / Auto-Renewable Subscriptions) exclusively for all iOS digital purchases.
• Advertising: Google AdMob SDK (with Apple App Tracking Transparency / SKAdNetwork) for displaying frequency-capped ads to free-tier users.
