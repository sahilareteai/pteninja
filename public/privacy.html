2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide intelligent scoring, personalized study roadmaps, and reliable access:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in via Google Authentication or Firebase Auth, we collect your name, email address, profile photo, and unique authentication identifier.
  • Voice and Audio Recordings (Microphone Access): When you practice Speaking tasks (such as Read Aloud, Repeat Sentence, Describe Image, Re-tell Lecture, and Answer Short Question), we record your voice when you tap the microphone button. Audio is recorded strictly on-demand.
  • Written Practice Responses: Text submitted for Writing and Reading questions (such as Summarize Written Text, Write Essay, Fill in the Blanks).
  • Progress and Exam Performance Data: Practice attempt scores, component breakdowns (fluency, pronunciation, content, grammar, vocabulary, spelling), mock test scorecards, and study goal records.
  • Purchase and Subscription Data: In-app billing status, plan identifiers, transaction timestamps, and order tokens processed through Google Play In-App Billing on mobile, or Stripe / Razorpay on the web. We never store or process raw payment card numbers.
  • Device and Usage Information: Device model, operating system version, app version, IP address (used temporarily for regional currency display), session diagnostics, and crash logs.
  • Advertising Identifiers (Mobile App): Android Advertising ID (AAID) processed by Google AdMob for ad delivery and analytics.

3. Microphone and Audio Data Usage

Because speaking assessment is a core part of PTE preparation, the mobile app requires the RECORD_AUDIO permission. We adhere to strict standards regarding your audio data:

  • On-Demand Recording Only: Audio is recorded only when you explicitly press the "Record" button during a speaking practice question or mock test. The app never records in the background.
  • Purpose Limitation: Audio recordings are used solely to assess your pronunciation, oral fluency, speech rate, and content accuracy against PTE scoring criteria.
  • No Commercial Sale: We do NOT sell, rent, or lease your audio recordings to third parties.
  • No Public AI Training: Your voice recordings are processed ephemerally and are NOT used to train public foundational AI models.

4. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Scoring

Our platform uses advanced AI models (including Google Gemini AI) to provide real-time, Pearson-calibrated feedback on speaking and writing tasks. Text and audio snippets sent to the AI processing pipeline are encrypted in transit via TLS/HTTPS and processed strictly for returning your scorecard.

8. International Privacy Rights & Compliances

A. European Economic Area & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you reside in the EEA or UK, you possess specific legal rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): Obtain confirmation and copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten", Art. 17): Have your personal data erased.
  • Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18): Restrict the processing of your data under specific conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object (Art. 21): Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

B. California Residents (CCPA / CPRA - United States)
